# NereidSphere Overview

NereidSphere is the thin suite layer of NereidAI: one portal, a single
sign-on (SSO) entry point, and a unified API gateway. It contains no product
business logic of its own.

## What you can do here

- **Sign in once** with your suite account on the NereidSphere login page.
- **Open any integrated product** from the product grid without signing in
  again: NereidLink, NereidForge and NereidHorizon.
- **Review your identity** — display name and roles are shown after login.

## The product grid

Each card links to one product. Clicking a card while you have a valid
session opens the product in a new window and keeps the portal available in
its original window. Product descriptions and common suite role labels follow
the selected portal language. See
[Opening products with SSO](help:page/product-sso).
